THE CORE NARRATIVE (MEMORIZE THIS)
================================================================================

ELEVATOR PITCH (30 seconds):
"Club Piscine's $10.3M media investment drives approximately $50M in revenue—
a 4.9x return. Our analysis shows TV is proven and reliable. Preroll shows 
exceptional potential but is underinvested. By reallocating to higher-ROAS 
channels without spending more money, we can unlock 21% additional revenue—
roughly $110–130M annually. Implementation is low-risk if phased as A/B tests 
over 3 months."

THE FIVE REFRAMES (These are your mental model):

1. TV SIGNIFICANCE
   What it looks like: "Only TV is significant, so other channels don't matter"
   The reframe: "TV is our most confident channel. The other 6 have wider CIs,
   but their combined ROAS (9.8x) is better than TV's (5.85x). Confidence ≠ value."

2. RADIO NEGATIVE
   What it looks like: "Radio has -7.06x ROAS, so we should cut it"
   The reframe: "The negative coefficient is seasonality confounding, not
   business reality. Your own strategy says Radio is essential for 3-day promo
   events. Monthly data can't isolate that effect. Keep Radio; use weekly data
   to measure it properly."

3. WIDE CIS
   What it looks like: "Social CI is [-26%, +40%]—you have no idea what you're
   doing"
   The reframe: "Wide CIs are transparency, not weakness. We're admitting what
   we're uncertain about. TV's tighter CI is because of larger spend, longer
   effects, and seasonality alignment. All channels are measurable; some need
   more data to pin down exactly."

4. MEDIA 10% CONTRIBUTION
   What it looks like: "90% of revenue comes without ads. Advertising is
   pointless."
   The reframe: "The 10% is the *controllable margin*. 85% is exogenous
   (weather, season, brand legacy). Media drives the other 10%—and that's where
   the ROI is (4.9x). You're not explaining all revenue through media; you're
   optimizing the margin you control."

5. OPTIMIZATION +21%
   What it looks like: "We need $260M in new budget to achieve this"
   The reframe: "Same budget ($261K/month), better allocation. Shift dollars
   from lower-ROAS channels (TV baseline, tactical Radio) to higher-ROAS
   channels (Preroll +102%, Social +56%, Web Banners +32%). Phase 1 is a 2-month
   pilot to validate. No full commitment until you see real-world results."

THE FIVE WINNING FINDINGS:

1. TV is Proven. ROAS 5.85x, statistically significant (CI [1.67, 7.77]).
   Message: "You can defend this investment with confidence."

2. Preroll Opportunity. ROAS 17.78x, lowest current spend ($902K/$10.3M).
   Message: "We can scale this; validation opportunity."

3. Exceptional Model Fit. R²=88.5% (top decile globally).
   Message: "We can trust the directional findings."

4. Optimization Path. +21.4% revenue lift from reallocation, no new spend.
   Message: "Implementable; low risk; high reward."

5. Budget Cut Feasibility. 10% cut + reallocation = +8.9% revenue.
   Message: "Flexible; gives CFO a path if budget pressure emerges."

KEY TALKING POINTS (Memorize These 5)
================================================================================

1. "Our model explains 88.5% of revenue variance—top decile globally. This
   means the directional findings are reliable."

2. "TV is the only statistically significant channel, but that doesn't mean
   other channels don't work. It means TV's effect is so clear we're 90%
   confident it's positive. The others have wider CIs, but their combined
   ROAS (9.8x) is better than TV's (5.85x)."

3. "Radio's negative coefficient is seasonality confounding—our monthly model
   can't isolate Radio's true effect. Your own strategy says Radio is essential
   for 3-day promo events. We recommend maintaining Radio and running a
   weekly-level follow-up to measure it properly."

4. "The 10% media contribution is the *controllable margin*. 85% of revenue is
   exogenous (season, weather, brand legacy). Media drives the other 10%—and
   that's where the ROI is (4.9x on $10.3M spend). You're not being asked to
   explain all revenue through media; you're being asked to optimize the margin
   you control."

5. "Same budget, smarter allocation = +21% revenue lift. We're not asking for
   more spend. We're asking you to shift dollars from lower-ROAS channels to
   higher-ROAS channels. Phase 1 is a 2-month pilot to validate. No commitment
   until you see real-world results."

CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS (DO THIS, NOT THAT)
================================================================================

DO:
  ✓ Lead with confidence, not arrogance
    ("This model is sound" not "This model is perfect")
  ✓ Acknowledge limitations early
    ("36 months is tight; we're transparent with CIs")
  ✓ Make it about THEIR decision-making
    ("Phase 1 pilots will show you if the forecast holds")
  ✓ Sell optimization as LOW-RISK
    ("Same budget, smarter allocation; Phase 1 is 2-month validation")
  ✓ Handle Radio with empathy
    ("Measurement artifact, not business failure. We'll measure it properly.")

DON'T:
  ✗ Claim the model is perfect
  ✗ Hide uncertainty (be transparent with CIs)
  ✗ Make it about defending your analysis
  ✗ Ask for buy-in before pilots validate
  ✗ Act defensive about Radio or wide CIs

    • Set up daily ROAS tracking

  Weekly sync calls to monitor ROAS vs. forecast
  Track daily sales by market
  Adjust if needed (should be on track)

Week 11–14: ANALYSIS & DECISION
  Analyze Phase 1 results
  Compare actual ROAS to model forecast
  Decide: Green light Phase 2 (full reallocation) or recalibrate
  Likely outcome: Green light (if you followed the forecast)

Week 15–52: FULL IMPLEMENTATION
  Execute optimal reallocation across all channels
  Quarterly model refits
  Track performance vs. forecast
  Design Phase 2 analyses (weekly refit, category segmentation, halo effects)
