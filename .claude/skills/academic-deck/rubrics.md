## Executive Summary Rubric (15% of total)

### Project Context (10% of exec summary)
- **Good**: Clear and concise context including industry relevance and key drivers. Reference to use cases' pain points.
- **Acceptable**: General context provided but lacks clarity and depth. Pain points minimally addressed.
- **Not Acceptable**: No clear context or relevance. No reference to client's use cases.

### Solution Overview (40% of exec summary)
- **Good**: Well-articulated summary with visual elements (e.g., screenshot). Indication of value drivers, modelling, KPI, UI/UX, and Technical Architecture.
- **Acceptable**: Basic summary; lacks visual support or clarity. Only some solution content indicated.
- **Not Acceptable**: No summary or unclear explanation. No indications of content.

### Solution Build and Analysis (40% of exec summary)
- **Good**: Clear overview and key elements of build. Insightful, well-supported review of data and analysis.
- **Acceptable**: Takeaways present but lack depth and connection to project.
- **Not Acceptable**: No key takeaways or irrelevant conclusions.

### Slide Design & Clarity (10% of exec summary)
- **Good**: Professionally designed, easy to read, visually engaging. All relevant content without overcrowding.
- **Acceptable**: Adequate design but may lack clarity or visual appeal. Somewhat disorganized/crowded.
- **Not Acceptable**: Poorly designed, overly cluttered, hard to read.

## Final Solution Deck Rubric (35% of total)

### Strategy / Value Proposition (15% of deck grade)
- **Good**: Comprehensive industry/market overview. Clear solution explanation. Clear strategic relevance (industry context, pain points, ROI). Strong use of external data and user scenarios. Benefits quantified and linked to client outcomes.
- **Acceptable**: General context but lacks depth. Minimal strategic relevance articulation. Limited external data/user scenarios. Benefits vaguely stated.
- **Not Acceptable**: No strategic context. No external support or user scenarios. No benefit analysis.

### Analytic Modeling (30% of deck grade)
- **Good**: Robust data prep, transformation, cleansing. Relevant data for analytical solutions. Robust and creative model approaches. Automated testing/validation where appropriate. Clear model explanation. Clear documentation. Assumptions clearly stated and supported. Clear link from modeling slides to video assets.
- **Acceptable**: Adequate data prep and modelling but lacks depth. Basic or common approaches. Limited validation/automated testing. Explanation lacks key elements. Documentation incomplete. Assumptions mentioned but unsupported. Video assets not effectively used.
- **Not Acceptable**: Poor/missing data prep. Weak/unjustified models. No documentation or video support. No explanation of assumptions.

### Solution Architecture (30% of deck grade)
- **Good**: Clearly solves key client issues. Considers data flow, scalability, automation. Future state with technical evolution path. Creative, justified, aligned tech choices. Scalability/automation/data flow addressed. Includes diagrams/visuals. Complex sections supported with video assets. **Architecture sufficiently mature for immediate industry-level deployment.**
- **Acceptable**: Solves basic client needs but lacks scalability. Architecture mentioned but lacks future-state vision. Adequate but not innovative tech choices. Only some sections have video support. Architecture requires extension before deployment.
- **Not Acceptable**: No clear architecture. No scalability/automation/data flow consideration. Unrealistic tech choices. No visuals or video. Architecture clearly unsuitable for deployment.

### User Adoption Strategy (25% of deck grade)
- **Good**: Strong focus on user needs, UI/UX design, stakeholder engagement. Description and justification of UX/UI design. Technical sophistication considered. Aesthetics/creativity leveraged. Stakeholders considered during UX/UI development. Video assets support stakeholder buy-in.
- **Acceptable**: UX/UI functional but lacks creativity or adoption rationale. Lacks technical sophistication. Aesthetics/creativity not leveraged enough. Lack of stakeholder consideration. Missing/low quality videos.
- **Not Acceptable**: No user adoption strategy or UI/UX relevance. No technical sophistication or aesthetics. No stakeholder consideration. No video support.

## Video Assets Rubric (20% of total)

### Content Relevance (60% of video grade)
- **Good**: Clearly complement slides. Chosen to explain complex aspects. Used sparingly, critically focused on key elements.
- **Acceptable**: Touch on relevant topics but lack depth or clarity. Some sections lack helpful video explanations. Lack focus on key elements.
- **Not Acceptable**: Irrelevant or don't support the project. No focus on key elements.

### Technical Explanation (20% of video grade)
- **Good**: Complex elements well-explained with visuals or examples.
- **Acceptable**: Some technical aspects explained but lack clarity.
- **Not Acceptable**: No technical explanation or unclear content.

### Presentation Quality (20% of video grade)
- **Good**: Professional delivery, clear audio/visuals. Engaging. Within time limit (<=4 min), well-integrated.
- **Acceptable**: Adequate delivery, minor clarity/engagement issues. Slightly overtime.
- **Not Acceptable**: Poor quality, hard to hear/see, disengaging. Over time limit or improperly formatted.

## Individual Interview Rubric (15% of total)

### Importance and value of capstone to clients (25%)
- **Good**: Clear understanding of project's purpose. Uses client-specific terminology. Effectively conveys principal added value with at least one concrete example.
- **Acceptable**: States purpose in general terms. Shares some understanding but without clear examples or relevance.
- **Not Acceptable**: Cannot articulate purpose or value. Vague or unrelated to client needs.

### Knowledge of project details (25%)
- **Good**: Deep understanding -- accurately describes data sources, architecture, modeling, UI. References decisions and rationale. Uses technical terminology correctly. Understands alignment with use cases and SOW.
- **Acceptable**: Mentions some components but omits key details or rationale. Lacks precision or technical depth. Little ability to explain SOW alignment.
- **Not Acceptable**: Cannot explain key components. Incorrect or superficial descriptions. Unable to explain technical aspects.

### Capstone connection to business context (25%)
- **Good**: Clearly connects design and implementation to business context. Links features to specific business objectives or KPIs. Explains how solution addresses pain points with industry/operations examples.
- **Acceptable**: General connections to business context. General explanation but can't link specific aspects to KPIs. Generic or unspecific examples.
- **Not Acceptable**: Fails to connect project to business needs, objectives or KPIs. Irrelevant, generic, incorrect or absent explanations.

### Overall understanding and individual contribution (25%)
- **Good**: Clear understanding of all report aspects. Full understanding of scope. Clearly articulates own role and responsibilities. Claims ownership of at least one complex element.
- **Acceptable**: Partial project understanding. Identifies contribution but lacks clarity or complexity. Difficulty taking ownership.
- **Not Acceptable**: Cannot explain or demonstrate clear understanding. Contribution and ownership unclear or minimal.

## Synthesis & Overall Performance (15% of total)

### Alignment Across Deliverables (25%)
- **Excellent**: All components tightly aligned in messaging, tone, content. Each part reinforces others.
- **Good**: Most components aligned; minor inconsistencies.
- **Acceptable**: Some alignment, gaps or contradictions.
- **Not Acceptable**: Disconnected or contradictory, lacking cohesion.

### Consistency of Technical Rigour (25%)
- **Excellent**: High technical detail and accuracy across all deliverables. Well-supported and clearly explained.
- **Good**: Generally strong; some areas lack depth or clarity.
- **Acceptable**: Inconsistent; some deliverables underdeveloped.
- **Not Acceptable**: Weak or inaccurate across multiple deliverables.

### Strategic & Stakeholder Relevance (25%)
- **Excellent**: Effectively addresses client needs, pain points, strategic goals. Stakeholder perspectives well-integrated.
- **Good**: Relevant, addresses key issues; stakeholder needs acknowledged.
- **Acceptable**: Underdeveloped, stakeholder needs minimally addressed.
- **Not Acceptable**: Lacks strategic relevance; stakeholder needs not considered.

### Professionalism & Organization (25%)
- **Excellent**: Professionally formatted, well-organized, visually engaging. Polished, easy to navigate. Clear file management.
- **Good**: Mostly professional; minor formatting issues. Files somewhat organized.
- **Acceptable**: Functional but lacks polish. Disorganized file management.
- **Not Acceptable**: Poor formatting, disorganized, difficult to follow. Files not organized.
