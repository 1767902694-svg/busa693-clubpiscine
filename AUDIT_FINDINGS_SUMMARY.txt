TOP 5 CRITICAL ISSUES
================================================================================

1. SAMPLE SIZE CRITICALLY SMALL (N=36 months)
   ──────────────────────────────────────────
   - Only 36 monthly observations across 3 fiscal years
   - 14 features / 36 obs = 2.6:1 ratio (standard = 5:1)
   - Effective DoF after Ridge shrinkage ≈ 20
   - Result: High parameter uncertainty, wide confidence intervals

   Evidence:
   - Stage 2 R² = 0.149 (media explains only 15% of residual variance)
   - 4 of 7 channels show zero coefficient (likely collinearity, not true zero)
   - Bootstrap CIs span 1-2 orders of magnitude (e.g., Radio: [0, 6010])
   - Preroll CI range: [14372, 38312] = 2.7x spread

   Risk: HIGH
   Mitigation:
   - Frame as "internal decision-support tool" not "definitive ROI measurement"
   - Use relative rankings (Preroll > TV > others) not absolute ROAS values
   - Acquire weekly data (156 observations available, currently unused)
   - Test recommendations in phases with monitoring


2. TV COEFFICIENT LIKELY UNDERSTATED (Multicollinearity)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   - TV spend heavily correlated with seasonality (r=0.62)
   - Ridge regression shrinks collinear predictors to prevent overfitting
   - Result: TV's measured 4.49x ROAS is likely conservative (understated)

   Evidence:
   - TV peaks same season as sales peak (Mar-Sep)
   - Cannot statistically separate TV effect from seasonal demand
   - TV is only 2 channels (with Preroll) passing all 4 robustness tests
   - Client narrative emphasizes TV's "halo effect" amplifying other channels
   - Two-stage design helps but doesn't fully eliminate collinearity

   Risk: MEDIUM
   Mitigation:
   - Enforce TV floor ($80K) in optimization (prevents over-cutting)
   - Document that TV's true ROI likely higher than 4.49x
   - Use TV coefficient for relative ranking, not absolute ROAS
   - Monitor brand metrics (awareness, consideration) alongside revenue


3. ZERO-ROAS CHANNELS: AMBIGUOUS INTERPRETATION
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────
   - Radio (23% of budget): ROAS=0, CI=[0, 6010]
   - Panneaux (3% of budget): ROAS=0, CI=[0, 63652]
   - Digital Flyers (5% of budget): ROAS=0, CI=[0, 36194]

   Could be:
   a) Truly ineffective → justified to cut
   b) Collinear with TV/Preroll → measured effect hidden by multicollinearity
   c) Seasonal effect mixed with controls → signal lost
   d) Insufficient sample size → can't detect weak signal

   Evidence:
   - Radio has 90% CI that includes zero (cannot rule out true effect)
   - Client narrative: Radio is "tactical mass medium, 3-day promo events Apr-Jul"
   - Digital Flyers: "conversion driver from mid-Jun onward" per client strategy
   - These are NOT evidence of ineffectiveness, but evidence of uncertainty

   Risk: MEDIUM-HIGH (if cuts are implemented without testing)
   Mitigation:
   - Label channels as "insufficient evidence of effect" not "proven ineffective"
   - Implement confidence-aware bounds (±20% of current) to prevent extreme cuts
   - Test recommendations with controlled experiments before full implementation
   - Monitor engagement metrics (CTR, visits) not just revenue


4. RESPONSE FUNCTIONS ARE POINT ESTIMATES, NOT DISTRIBUTIONS
   ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   - Optimization uses single coefficient per channel
   - Ignores uncertainty in coefficient estimates
   - Treats Preroll ROAS=27.7x as certain when CI=[14.4, 38.3]

   Result:
   - Optimizer allocates as if coefficients are known without error
   - If true Preroll ROAS is 14x (lower CI), 102% increase may be suboptimal
   - No Monte Carlo sensitivity to coefficient uncertainty

   Risk: MEDIUM
   Mitigation:
   - Document that optimization is "based on point estimates with wide uncertainty"
   - Perform scenario analysis: re-optimize at lower ROAS assumptions
   - Recommend phased implementation (not all-or-nothing reallocation)
   - Use confidence bounds to constrain uncertain channels


5. NON-NEGATIVE CONSTRAINT MASKS POTENTIAL OVERSATURATION
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   - NNLS forces all coefficients ≥ 0
   - Assumes advertising cannot reduce sales
   - Cannot show if channel is saturated or has negative returns

   Example:
   - If Preroll truly faces diminishing returns above $40K, model shows linear
   - If creative quality issues exist, model attributes to zero not negative coefficient

   Risk: LOW-MEDIUM (low for new channels, medium for mature ones)
   Mitigation:
   - Monitor saturation curves empirically (weekly spend vs ROI)
   - Test Preroll at increased levels with A/B control
   - Document that zero coefficient does NOT mean oversaturation, just uncertainty
