## CRITICAL TALKING POINTS (Memorize These)

**Point 1: Model Quality**
"Our model explains 88.5% of revenue variance—top decile globally. This means we can trust its directional findings."

**Point 2: TV Significance**
"TV is the only statistically significant channel, but that doesn't mean other channels don't work. It means TV's effect is so clear that we're 90% confident it's positive. The others have wider confidence intervals, but their combined ROAS (9.8x) is better than TV's (5.85x)."

**Point 3: Radio Negative (The Most Sensitive)**
"Radio's negative coefficient is due to seasonality confounding—our monthly model can't isolate Radio's true effect. Your own strategy says Radio is essential for 3-day promo events. We recommend maintaining Radio and running a weekly-level follow-up to measure it properly."

**Point 4: Media Contribution 10% (The "Is This Bad?" Question)**
"The 10% is the *controllable margin*. 85% of revenue is exogenous (season, weather, brand legacy). Media drives the other 10%—and that's where the ROI is (4.9x). You're not being asked to explain all revenue through media; you're being asked to optimize the margin you control."

**Point 5: Optimization Action**
"Same budget, smarter allocation = +21% revenue lift. We're not asking for more spend. We're asking you to shift dollars from lower-ROAS channels (TV baseline, tactical radio) to higher-ROAS channels (Preroll, Social, Web Banners). Phase 1 is a 2-month pilot to validate. No commitment until you see real-world results."
