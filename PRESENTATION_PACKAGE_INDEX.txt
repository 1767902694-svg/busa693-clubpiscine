CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS
================================================================================

1. Lead with confidence, not arrogance
   → "This model is sound" (not "perfect")
   → "These findings are reliable" (not "guaranteed")

2. Acknowledge limitations early
   → "36 months is tight; we're being transparent with CIs"
   → "Monthly data obscures event-level effects (Radio); weekly refit will fix"

3. Make it about THEIR decision-making
   → "Phase 1 pilots will show you whether the forecast holds"
   → "Real-world results will validate the model"

4. Sell optimization as LOW-RISK
   → "Same budget, smarter allocation"
   → "Phase 1 is a 2-month validation test"

5. Handle Radio with empathy
   → "Negative coefficient = measurement artifact, not business failure"
   → "Your own strategy says Radio is essential; trust that. We'll measure it properly."
