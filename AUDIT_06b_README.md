## Audit Scores Summary

### Methodology & Implementation: ✅ Good
- ✅ Frisch-Waugh two-stage decomposition correctly implemented
- ✅ Non-negative constraint via NNLS augmented matrix is sound
- ✅ Bootstrap methodology is standard
- ✅ Code quality is good (clean, well-documented)

### Statistical Power: ❌ Poor
- ❌ N=36 observations, p=7-12 parameters (ratio 3:1 vs 5:1 standard)
- ❌ Adjusted R² negative for media-only model (5/7 products)
- ❌ Media explains only 3% of total revenue variance
- ❌ Wide confidence intervals (factor of 2-10× coefficient magnitude)

### Model Results: ⚠️ Directional Only
- ✅ Ranking (Preroll > Social > Web > TV) is likely correct directionally
- ⚠️ Magnitude (ROAS values) are unreliable
- ⚠️ Only 2 channels statistically significant (TV, Preroll)
- ⚠️ 3 channels zeroed (Radio, Panneaux, Circulaire) due to weak signal + constraint

### Key Assumptions: ⚠️ Questionable
- ⚠️ TV decay (λ=0.2) is low; 3.2× sensitivity to decay assumption
- ⚠️ Saturation K values are data-driven heuristics, not optimized
- ⚠️ No causal design; observational data only
- ⚠️ Single 3-year window; regime shift risk

## Critical Findings at a Glance

| Finding | Severity | Evidence | Implication |
|---------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Only 36 observations** | 🔴 Critical | N/p = 3:1 vs 5:1 standard | Statistical power insufficient |

## Conclusion

Notebook 06b is a **competent but under-powered model** that answers the question "which channels appear to work best?" but not "what is the true ROI of each channel?" With only 36 observations, high seasonality, and multicollinearity, individual channel effects cannot be reliably estimated. The results are directionally useful for strategic thinking (invest in Preroll, test Social), but NOT for tactical budget optimization.

**Primary Limitation:** Sample size (N=36) relative to model complexity (7 channels + controls).

**Primary Strength:** Proper statistical methodology with careful constraint handling.

**Recommendation:** Use this as exploratory analysis to inform a larger research program (weekly data, experimental validation). Do not rely on it exclusively for client budget decisions.
