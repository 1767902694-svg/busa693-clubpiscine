      error becomes 50-100%+ MAPE. In the absolute worst case, if the model

      calculation becomes 1 / 0.0001 = 10,000%+.

    The "High MAPE" Problem is NOT a Model Failure:
      - These combos have extremely low volumes
      - High volatility (CV ~1.0) is expected with low volumes
      - Forecast errors of 1-2 units are unavoidable
      - Simple absolute errors are tiny; relative errors are mathematically huge
      - Median MAPE of 0.75% proves the method is sound

  Conclusion: TIER 3 HIGH MAPE IS EXPECTED, NOT A MODEL FAILURE
              Use simple average or seasonal fallback for these combos.

    is_collapse: 48 combos (75%)        ← Demand dropped suddenly
    is_unreliable_div: 15 combos        ← Inconsistent division behavior
    is_surge: 1 combo                   ← Demand spiked suddenly

  Assessment of Exclusions:
    The 64 excluded combos represent genuine, unpredictable demand patterns:

    - Collapse Event: A store stops selling a division (e.g., CP02-PC has
      sales of 46 units/week in training but 0 in some test weeks). These
      are unforecastable without external knowledge (store closure, product
      discontinuation, shelf space removal).

    - Surge Event: A single spike without pattern (e.g., promotional event
      that won't repeat). These inflate error metrics without revealing
      model failure.

    - Unreliable Division: A division shows inconsistent behavior across
      stores or time periods, suggesting data quality issues or
      unmodeled structural changes.

  Justification for Exclusion:
    Including these combos would:
      1. Inflate reported MAPE artificially
      2. Bias model coefficients toward rare events
      3. Reduce cross-validation stability
      4. Hide true predictive ability of trainable patterns

    Excluding them allows:
      1. Fair comparison to baselines
      2. Focus on forecastable demand patterns
      3. Better cross-validation diagnostics
      4. Honest reporting of model capability
