VERDICT: XGBoost is solid but Prophet fallback never materializes. Tiered 
strategy is conservative but leaves 2/3 of the architecture unused.

8. CROSS-VALIDATION: TIME-SERIES DONE CORRECTLY
================================================================================

CV Strategy: Expanding-window (walk-forward)

Configuration:
  N_CV_FOLDS = 3
  EVAL_HORIZON = 1 week
  MIN_HISTORY = 6 weeks

Implementation:
  Fold 1: Train weeks 1-8,   Test week 9
  Fold 2: Train weeks 1-9,   Test week 10
  Fold 3: Train weeks 1-10,  Test week 11
  
  (All training on Tier 1 groups with 14+ weeks history)

✓ STRENGTHS:
  - No look-ahead bias (strictly historical training)
  - Respects temporal order (no shuffling)
  - Expanding window simulates production scenario
  - Conservative 1-week holdout (tight evaluation)
  - Multiple folds provide robust estimates

✗ WEAKNESSES:
  - Only 3 folds (could use 8+ for longer series)
  - 1-week holdout is very tight (limits test signal)
  - No stratification by store/season (seasonal stores mixed)
  - No stability testing (fold-to-fold variation not reported)

VERDICT: PROPER implementation. CV is rigorous and follows time-series 
best practices. More folds would be even better but 3 is acceptable.
